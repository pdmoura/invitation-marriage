# Surface: guest invitation page (public/index.html)

Mode: Persuade (the guest confirms attendance and gives a gift). Audience: invited guests on Android phones via WhatsApp, PT/EN/ES.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is Pedro & Sara's printed invitation, not a wedding website. The envelope opens to the card; every section is another piece of the same stationery (letter, day cards, gift tags, reply card, back of the card with a Formosa stamp). Refuses the hero-photo + floating nav + centred sections template.

OWN-WORLD: Slate ground #a6b3bc with paper panels; sage, blush and slate tone fields; deep olive #2c3620 for actions and the close. Bebas Neue capitals for names and numbers, EB Garamond for every sentence. Filled shapes with real edges (scallops, dotted perforations, notches), square corners, no hairlines, no gradients, no shadows at rest.

STORY: The guest sees their own name on the envelope, opens it, reads the date and place in the first screen, confirms in one tap on the reply card, and gives a gift through a swing-tagged tile or one contribution card.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop split 50/50 with 12px gutters: left the invitation card (olive/paper striped ribbon band on top, PEDRO & SARA in Bebas at full card width, date and Formosa in Garamond, the guest's name, three round icon actions: Confirmar presença, Lista de presentes, Local); right a full-height sticky photograph that slowly crossfades. Phone: photo 46svh, card overlapping it by 24px.
