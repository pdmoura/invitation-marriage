## Brazilian gift-list conventions

- Brazilian cash registries (iCasei, Festeggiare, Casar.com, Quero de Casamento) all accept **Pix + card**, but take a commission on card payments and hold the money for days. Running our own Pix QR removes the middleman for Pix. ([my-best comparison](https://br.my-best.com/20640), [Festeggiare help](https://intercom.help/festeggiare/pt-BR/articles/8754951-convidados-aquisicao-de-presentes))
- Funny "cotas" and Pix jokes are now common and well received. ([Casamentos.com.br — Pix de casamento](https://www.casamentos.com.br/artigos/pix-de-casamento--c10086))

## What we took from all this

1. **Typography first** — oversized serif names (Cormorant Garamond) + clean sans (DM Sans).
2. **Warm neutral palette** — cream, olive green, terracotta, blush. No pure white backgrounds.
3. **Arch-framed photo** — the most repeated motif on Pinterest/Dribbble in 2025–26.
4. **Countdown + clear CTAs** — "Confirmar presença" and "Lista de presentes" above the fold.
5. **Boutique-style gift shop** — category chips, product cards, cart drawer; feels like a store, but every "product" is just money.
6. **Mobile-first** — 2-column gift grid and a full-height drawer on phones.
